How the day goes.

A typical full day, eight hours or longer on the water. Nothing is fixed: ask for changes and agree the route, stops and timing with your skipper.

    1. Meet and greet

      Welcome aboard in Omiš. We introduce ourselves, show you around the boat and talk through the day, so you can tell us what you would like.

    2. Across the Brač Channel

      Settle in with a drink as we cruise along the Dalmatian coast, the mountains behind you and Brač ahead.

    3. Swim and snorkel

      We drop anchor in a secluded cove. Swim, snorkel along the rocks, or simply float and do nothing at all.

    4. Visit the town of Pučišća

      Step ashore in one of the most beautiful stone villages in Croatia, built from the white limestone Brač is famous for, around a calm harbour.

    5. Paddleboard and water toys

      Take the SUP board out and test your balance, grab a snorkel, or play with the water toys in a quiet bay.

    6. Local delicacies

      A cold plate of prosciutto, cheese, pancetta and other local specialities with seasonal fruit, served on deck with drinks.

    7. Postira or Splitska

      A stop in a traditional Dalmatian village. Have a coffee by the sea, or lunch at a restaurant on the waterfront.

    8. One last swim

      Before heading back we anchor in another secluded cove for a final swim and a few drinks.

    9. Back to Omiš

      Sit back and watch the coast slide past on the way home. A good end to a day on the water.

    Good to know.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Cancellation is free at any time, and there is no deposit.

    How do I pay?

    You pay on the day of the trip. Nothing is charged when you send a request.

    What if the weather is bad?

    We move your trip to another day or cancel it for free. Your choice.

    Where do we meet?

    In Omiš harbour. Pickups from nearby places can be arranged on request.

    Do I need a boat licence?

    No. A skipper is always on board and included in the price.

    Can we choose where to go?

    Yes. The route is yours. Tell us what you like and we plan the day around it.

    What about a half day?

    Same idea with fewer stops. You and your skipper pick the ones you like most.

    What should we bring?

    Swimwear, a towel and sunscreen. Food and drinks are on us.
