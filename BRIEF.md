# Amphora Boat: brief

Interviewed (grilling session with the owner, Nov 2026). Decisions are his unless marked.

- **What it is:** one boat (Beneteau Antares 9) in Omiš, Croatia. Private skippered day trips, pickups in Split. About 90% tourists.
- **What the visitor must believe:** this is an easy, all-inclusive private day, and asking costs nothing.
- **The one action:** "Check dates" (calendar, then a pre-filled WhatsApp request; email as backup).
- **Offer:** full day €650 (starts 10:00), guest email required on requests. Half day €450 (8:00 to 13:00 or 14:00 to 19:00). Departures from Omiš, nearby pickups on request; Split pickup to come with the SEO update. Prices marked * (big route changes or special requests can change them). Up to 10 guests. Free cancellation anytime, pay on the day. No direct-booking perk.
- **Languages:** English, German, Croatian. One page each.
- **Reference:** a book whose pages turn. Used in one place only.
- **Assets:** logo, one Omiš photo, one fast-cut social clip (stills taken from it as stand-ins). Nothing generated.
- **Structure:** hero, price, the day as a story, the boat, reviews, calendar and booking, FAQ, close. A pinned Book bar on phones.

## Feeling curve
1. Hero: arrival. The boat already at anchor under the cliffs.
2. Price: relief. One number for the whole boat.
3. The day (peak): anticipation. The day turns page by page like a logbook.
4. The boat: trust. What it is, plainly.
5. Booking: control. Free days visible, nothing charged.
6. Close: ease. Pick your day.

**Peak:** the logbook. "It's the site where the day turns like the pages of a logbook."

**Signature move:** the scroll-turned logbook (bespoke, in site.js). Under reduced motion it becomes a plain list of chapters.

**Authored (mine, not the owner's words):** all copy, the palette, the chapter times of day.
